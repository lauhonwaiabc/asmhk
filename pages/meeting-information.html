Meeting Information

Venue, travel and practical details for ASM 2027 at the Hopewell Hotel, Wan Chai.

Meeting and Exhibition

The Annual Scientific Meeting in Anaesthesiology 2027 (ASM 2027) and its Exhibition will be held on 30–31 October 2027 at the Hopewell Hotel. Pre-conference workshops will be held on 29 October 2027 in Salon 1–2, Crystal Court, Hopewell Hotel.

Meeting Venue

Crystal Court, 19/F and Salons, 20/F
Hopewell Hotel

Address:15 Kennedy Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong
Tel:(852) 2620 3000
Website:www.hopewellhotel.com

How to go to Hopewell Hotel
Please view the location map of Hopewell Hotel.

Pre-conference Workshops

The pre-conference workshops will be held on 29 October 2027 in Salon 1–2, Crystal Court, Hopewell Hotel. The pre-conference programme includes PBL Workshops 1 and 2. Registration for these workshops is separate from the main meeting and places are limited.

Stamp Collection & souvenir

Every symposium, session and workshop has its own QR code displayed in the room. Open the ASM 2027 event app, choose Stamp Collection, and scan the session code. There are eight eligible slots across Day 1 and Day 2; lunch symposia and ceremonies are excluded. Parallel sessions in the same slot count as one stamp. Collect at least 7 of the 8 slot stamps to claim a complimentary ASM 2027 souvenir at the souvenir desk.

Official language of the Meeting is English.

Certificate of Attendance

An Evaluation Survey will be sent to the attendees to collect their valuable feedbacks after the Meeting. E-Certificate of Attendance will be emailed to those who filled out the Evaluation after the Meeting.
