Call for Free Paper and e-Poster

Every compliant abstract is accepted as an e-Poster. At submission, choose e-Poster only or e-Poster plus consideration for the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation.

Free Paper and e-Poster

One abstract. Two presentation options.

Judges review all accepted e-Posters at the Day 1 e-Poster Evaluation Session on Saturday 30 October 2027, 14:00–15:30, Pre-function area. The shortlist for the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation on Sunday 31 October 2027, 11:00–12:30, Salon 1–2 is announced on site after the evaluation.
