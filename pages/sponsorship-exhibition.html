Sponsorship Package

The Organising Committee welcomes the support of the healthcare industry. ASM 2027 brings together anaesthesiologists, trainees, nurses and allied health professionals from Hong Kong, the mainland and the region for two days of scientific exchange and hands-on teaching, with pre-conference workshops on 29 October 2027.

Companies interested in supporting the meeting, taking a lecture or workshop slot, or exhibiting are invited to contact the Organising Committee at asmhkorg@gmail.com.

Sponsorship package 2027 (PDF) Discuss a package

Why partner with ASM 2027

  • Direct access to a national and regional audience of anaesthesia and perioperative specialists.
  • Named lecture, symposium or workshop opportunities aligned to the scientific programme.
  • Exhibition space alongside the main meeting rooms and catering area.
  • Recognition in the e-programme, website and on-site signage, in line with the agreed package.
  • Multi-year options for partners who wish to build a continuing relationship with the meeting.

Sponsorship packages

Exhibition

The healthcare industry exhibition runs alongside the scientific sessions at the Hopewell Hotel. Twenty booths are planned across the exhibition area.

  • Twenty booths, including premium and standard positions.
  • Booth allocation is confirmed on receipt of the signed contract.
  • The exhibitor manual, build-up and dismantling times are issued six weeks before the meeting.

The venue floor plans for 19/F and 20/F are on the Meeting Information page.

Using the app on site. Open the event app, choose Sponsors to see confirmed partners and exhibitor details.
