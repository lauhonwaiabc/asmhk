Welcome Message

Theme: Anaesthesia Intelligence

Intelligence in anaesthesia is not only artificial.

The 2027 theme looks beyond the technology cycle. Artificial intelligence is entering monitoring, decision support and education, but its value depends on the clinical intelligence of the people and systems around it. The meeting will examine what is ready for practice, what still needs evidence, and how we keep judgement at the centre of care.

01

Artificial intelligence

Decision support, intelligent monitoring, closed-loop delivery and the evidence that should accompany them.

02

Human intelligence

Clinical judgement, human factors, team performance and the education that prepares us for new tools.

03

System intelligence

Data, governance, implementation and the design of reliable care across departments and borders.
